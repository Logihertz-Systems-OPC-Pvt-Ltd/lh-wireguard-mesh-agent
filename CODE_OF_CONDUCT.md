# Code of Conduct: Logihertz Systems

## Our Pledge

Logihertz Systems (OPC) Private Limited pledges to provide a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free environment for all participants in our open-source projects, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected Standards

Participants are expected to:
- Demonstrate empathy and kindness toward other members of the community.
- Respect differing opinions, viewpoints, and technical experiences.
- Give and gracefully accept constructive technical critique.
- Focus on what is best for the overall security and resilience of the community.

## Enforcement & Reporting

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project administrators at `ops@logihertz.com`. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
